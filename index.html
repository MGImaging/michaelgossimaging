About MGI

Exterior 3D imaging for outdoor living

Michael Goss Imaging (MGI) is an exterior-only 3D landscape rendering studio. The work helps homeowners, pool builders, and outdoor living contractors evaluate the finished outdoor space before construction.

Projects are modeled in Structure Studios VIP 3D. Each engagement begins with the house and site, then the landscape and outdoor design, so the renders remain grounded in the actual property.

  • Exterior landscape and outdoor living visualization only
  • Collaboration with pool builders, hardscape contractors, builders, and homeowners
  • Still images and optional walkthrough video for proposals and design decisions
