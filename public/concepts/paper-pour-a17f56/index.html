How a visit goes

  1. Order at the counter

    The list is on the board and on this page. Say if you want oat milk or the coffee black.

  2. We brew it

    Espresso drinks are pulled to order. Pour-over takes a few extra minutes, and we will say so.

  3. Stay or walk

    There are seats by the window. Cups also leave in a paper sleeve if you are already late.

Find the shop

42 Example Lane, on the ground floor. We serve the block and anyone walking through it. Hours are Tuesday to Friday, 7 to 3, and Saturday and Sunday, 8 to 3. Monday the shop is dark.

Questions

Do you roast the coffee?

No. We buy from roasters and brew it here. The board names the coffee that is open that week.

Is there a table for a group?

The largest table seats six. For a morning that needs the whole room, write us and we will say if a weekday before open is free.

What about milk and gluten?

Oat milk is always on the bar. Pastries are made with wheat. The yogurt cup is the plate without a pastry.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, the name and password are on a card at the counter. The shop is still a coffee counter first.
