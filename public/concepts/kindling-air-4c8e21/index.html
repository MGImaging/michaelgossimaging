Heating and cooling

Quiet heat. Steady cool.

Kindling Air looks after furnaces, air conditioners, and heat pumps for houses in the north neighborhoods and the streets nearby. We start with what the system is doing, then say what a repair or a replacement would involve.

Request a visit

What we work on

Four kinds of visits cover most houses. If the job is outside that list, we say so before anyone is on the schedule.

Heating

Furnaces that will not start, blow cool air, or short-cycle. We also replace a furnace when repair no longer makes sense for the house.

Cooling

Air conditioners that freeze, leak at the indoor coil, or cannot keep up on a hot afternoon. Replacement includes matching the outdoor unit to the indoor coil.

Heat pumps

One system for both seasons. We check refrigerant, coils, and the changeover between heat and cool, then explain what we found in plain language.

Airflow

Thermostats, filters, and ducts that leave a room stale or noisy. A small airflow problem is often the whole complaint.

How a visit goes

  1. Call

    Tell us the symptom and, if you can see it, the name on the equipment. We set a time that is actually open.

  2. Look

    On site we watch the system run, check the obvious failures, and stop if the safe next step is a part we do not have with us.

  3. Quote

    You get the work in writing before we start a repair or order equipment. The quote names the equipment and the labor.

  4. Finish

    After the work, we walk through the thermostat settings with you and leave the area swept.

Where we drive

Questions

Do you handle both heat and air conditioning?

Yes. Furnaces, central air conditioners, and heat pumps are the core of the work. We do not take on plumbing or electrical projects that are separate from the heating and cooling system.

What should I have ready when I call?

A short description of what changed, and the brand on the indoor or outdoor unit if it is easy to read. Photos of the thermostat screen help, and they are optional.

Can I book a seasonal check?

Yes. A check is a scheduled visit to run the system, replace a filter if you have one on hand, and note anything that should be watched. It is not a promise that nothing will fail later.

How fast can someone come?

We book the next open visit. We do not promise a same-day arrival.
