Dental office

Dentistry explained in plain words.

Birchroom is a general dental office for exams, cleanings, and the repairs that follow. We say what we see, what it means, and what can wait.

Request a visit

Care we provide

Exams and cleanings

A look at teeth and gums, cleaning, and a conversation about what to watch. X-rays are taken when they add something the eye cannot see.

Fillings

A small repair when decay is limited to part of a tooth. We numb the area, remove the decay, and fill the space.

Crowns

When a tooth needs more than a filling, a crown covers what remains. The plan, including a temporary, is explained before we start.

Tooth pain

Call if a tooth hurts, breaks, or swells. We fit an urgent visit into the book when a chair is open that day. We do not promise a same-hour seat.

What a first visit includes

  1. Paperwork

    Health history and the reason you came. Bring a list of medicines if you have one.

  2. Exam

    We look, we listen, and we take images only where they change the decision.

  3. A plan you can read

    You leave with what we recommend now, what can wait, and the order we would do it.

  4. The next chair

    Treatment is a separate visit unless the repair is small and you want it the same day.

The office

300 Sample Boulevard. Birchroom sees patients from the nearby neighborhoods. The door is at street level. If you need a quieter appointment, ask for the first chair of the day.

Questions

What should I bring?

A photo ID, your health history, and any dental plan card you want us to note. We can record the plan. Coverage itself is between you and the plan.

I get nervous in the chair.

Tell us. We explain each step before it happens, and we stop if you raise a hand. There is no extra performance around that.

How often is a cleaning?

Many people come twice a year. Gums that need closer watching may be asked back sooner. We say why.

Do you whiten teeth?

We can talk through take-home trays when the teeth are healthy enough for them. Whitening does not repair decay, and the shade change varies.
