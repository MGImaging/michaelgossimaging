A family bakery

Casa Monarca is Adriana Heredia’s shop. She and her daughter-in-law, Brianna Gil, named it together, after the monarch butterfly. Adriana says that migration matches her own move in search of a new life.

She taught herself to bake after moving to Dickinson in 2013, first from a cottage kitchen called A'mores Pastry. The shop on 2nd Avenue East opened in May 2025.

Who you will meet

Adriana Heredia bakes the pastries, flan, cakes, and coffee.

Brianna Gil makes the boba, lattes, and other flavored drinks.
