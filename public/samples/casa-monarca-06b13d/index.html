A family shop

Casa Monarca is Adriana Heredia’s bakery. She and her daughter-in-law, Brianna Gil, named the shop together, after the monarch butterfly.

Adriana taught herself to bake after she moved to Dickinson in 2013. She sold from a cottage kitchen called A'mores Pastry before the shop opened in May 2025.

Adriana Heredia

Owner and baker. Her case holds Mexican-style pastries, flan, cakes, and coffee.

Brianna Gil

Co-owner. She makes the boba, lattes, and other flavored drinks.

Custom cakes

Custom cakes need about a week’s notice. Call the shop to place an order.
