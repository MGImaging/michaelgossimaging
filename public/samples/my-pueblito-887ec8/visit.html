What can I buy?

Products from Mexico: candies, hibiscus tea, peppers, spices, corn husks, Mexican bread, personal care items, supplements, traditional clothing, sandals, and pottery.

Is there food?

Yes. The taqueria grew from a cart into a food trailer, and there is a dining room in the shop.
