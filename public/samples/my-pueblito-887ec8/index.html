Groceries, candy, and a taqueria in Dickinson.

My Pueblito is a Mexican market and taqueria owned by Erika Vasquez and Raul Guerrero. The shop is in Suite D at 11 2nd Avenue East.

Call (701) 300-1190

The market

They opened the market in Dickinson in 2020. The shelves carry products from Mexico: food, candy, personal care items, and supplements.

The taqueria

The food side started as a cart. It is now a food trailer, with dining at the shop.
