Divisions

Manufacturing & fabrication

On-site welding and fabrication, certified pipe and structural steel, stainless and aluminum, prefab packages, pressure vessel manufacturing and repairs.

Pressure vessels

ASME U-Stamp manufacturing and National Board R-Stamp repair for industrial and energy work.

Prefab skid & pipe packages

Shop-built skids: engineering review, fabrication, mechanical and electrical assembly, testing, and field support. We mobilize across North Dakota, Montana, and surrounding regions.

Robotic & custom welding

Carbon steel, stainless, and aluminum welding. Robotic welding up to 5× faster on carbon steel assemblies, a repair rate under 1%, and recorded weld parameters.

Plasma cutting & flanges

CNC plasma cutting, plate forming, and an in-house pipe lap-joint flange system. Prefabrication can cut site cost 20–30%.

Sandblasting & coating

Abrasive sandblasting, painting, spray foam, thermal spray, coating, internal pipe coating, concrete mudjacking, in the shop and in the field.

Energy services

Construction and maintenance crews available 24/7. Work includes tank batteries, dirt and berm work, pumping-unit maintenance, well-site maintenance, helical piers, compressor and gas lift, snow removal, lease operating, and environmental cleanup.
