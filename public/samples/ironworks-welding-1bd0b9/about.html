About

Locally owned in western North Dakota

Ironworks says it was founded in 2005 by someone with a passion for welding, and that it has been locally owned and operated since 2005, specializing in Bakken oilfield services. The company says sister companies became the divisions it runs now.

A Dickinson Press profile identifies Mike Gayda, a Dickinson native, as the person who started Iron Works Welding. That article describes him starting in 2006 with one service truck. The company site says 2005. Both are public; this page does not pick a headcount from the older article.

Carrier record

Operating authority is shown as not authorized for for-hire interstate work; the record is marked private property and intrastate, non-hazmat. That filing lists 26 power units and 26 drivers. Those counts are the filing, not a current payroll claim.
