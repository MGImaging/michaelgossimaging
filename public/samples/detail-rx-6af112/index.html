What is in the paint

Swirls

Fine marks that dull the clear coat. Light swirls are the Enhancement Correction, starting at $495.

Oxidation

Haze and neglected paint need more than a single polish. That is the Multi-Stage Correction, starting at $1,095.

Scratches

Light scratches in the clear can polish out. Scratches through the clear are touch-up work with Dr. ColorChip, quoted on inspection.

After the coating

General care, not a shop rulebook. Coatings booked here include a free hand wash four times a year.

  • Skip automatic brush washes. The brushes put swirls back into the finish.
  • Wash with a pH-neutral soap and a clean mitt. Dry with clean towels.
  • Give a fresh coating an easy first wash. Do not compound or polish over it.
  • Bird droppings and bugs come off sooner if they are not left to bake.
  • Use the four included hand washes to keep the coating clear.
